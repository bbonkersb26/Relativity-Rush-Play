Controls

Swipe left or right to change lanes. At a marked corner, swipe toward the road's bend. Turn instructions stop after your first completed turn. Swipe up to jump and down to slide; actions begin during the gesture. At a multiverse fork, pick a lane leading into a colored wormhole. How to play includes scene previews.

Game Center leaderboard

In the iPhone and iPad app, open Leaderboard and choose Connect to Game Center to share your nickname and best actual distance in whole metres with other players. Only new non-practice runs begun after connecting are submitted; earlier saved records stay on your device. The leaderboard shows the global top 100 and your own rank when available. If a submission is interrupted, the best pending distance stays on your device for the same Game Center account. Reconnect and open or refresh Leaderboard to retry. Distances go directly to Apple, not our wallet server. Deleting your store account or clearing local data does not erase Apple Game Center records; manage those records through Apple.

Missing purchases

The public Safari playtest has purchases disabled. In a native build with purchases available, connect to the internet and choose Restore purchases in the Store to verify eligible permanent purchases, such as the Cosmic violet suit. Verification may take a moment. Restore purchases does not replace recovery of your original paid wallet. If you lose access to that wallet after reinstalling or on another device, use your private recovery code to recover its purchased Quanta, upgrades and charges before making further purchases. A local progress export contains earned progress and is separate from the purchased wallet. Contact support if you cannot recover access; never email your recovery code.

Optional ad rewards

Rewarded ads are available only in enabled native builds. Normal runs of at least 100 m may offer one ad continue and an end-of-run bonus equal to 25% of collected Quanta, rounded down and capped at 50. There is no daily limit on continues. The bonus requires at least four collected Quanta and has no daily limit, but can be earned only once per eligible finished run. Practice, Daily, weekly and friend challenges do not receive these run offers. You can always end a run without watching. No ad availability or fill is guaranteed. Closing an ad without earning its reward does not grant a reward.

Ad and saved continues

In an enabled native build, a normal run of at least 100 m can use one ad continue and one saved paid continue, in either order, for up to two continues in the same run. Each source can be used only once in that run. A pack adds 3 saved continues after the purchase is verified. The US price is $2.99; your device shows the localized store price before you buy. Buying a pack does not resume the run or spend a continue automatically: choose Use 1 continue to resume. Unused paid continues stay in your purchased wallet for future eligible runs. Practice, Daily, weekly and friend challenges do not offer continues.

Single-use charge rewards

In Gear → Supplies → Watch for a charge, choose Shield, Magnetar or Slow time. Each verified ad grants one single-use earned charge for that gadget, without activating it. The limit is 3 charges per account per UTC day, shared across all three gadgets and resetting at 00:00 UTC. This offer is independent of run distance. Its daily charge allowance is separate from run continues and end-of-run bonuses, which have no daily cap. The displayed remaining allowance includes active pending reservations. Cancelling an unearned request releases its reservation once cancellation is confirmed; an unconfirmed request also releases it when it expires. Failed or unavailable ads grant nothing. Charge ads never grant permanent upgrades, outfits or characters.

A reward is still pending

Wait until the ad closes. If verification or saving is interrupted, use Check reward on the run screen, Check saved reward in Gear → Supplies, or Settings → Check ad rewards for pending bonuses and charges. A pending request is not a confirmed reward: unconfirmed requests expire after 15 minutes and release any reserved charge allowance. Cancelled unearned requests release it once cancellation is confirmed. Keep app data to recover rewards already confirmed by the server but not yet saved locally. Each gadget holds up to 99 earned charges. If stock becomes full before a confirmed charge is saved, use an earned charge and check the pending reward again; do not watch another ad for it. A continue can resume only its original run; it cannot restore a run after restarting the app or ending that run. Settings can discard ended-run continues without discarding pending bonuses or charges. Ad bonuses add earned Quanta only and do not multiply mission rewards or paid-wallet funds.

Performance and sound

Choose Balanced graphics in Settings if the device becomes hot or the game stutters. Reduced ambient motion lowers decorative effects. Start a run or tap Sound to enable audio. Pause the game before switching apps.

Report a problem

Include your device model, operating system version, game version, what happened, and the steps that trigger it. A screenshot can help. Do not include passwords, payment card details, or recovery codes.
