Preview, purchases and optional ads

Purchases and rewarded ads are disabled in the public Safari playtest. Native purchases and optional rewarded ads are available only in builds where those services are enabled. The service details below apply when you use them.

Progress on your device

Scores, earned Quanta, settings, character unlocks, and saved runs are stored on your device. Export progress in Settings before clearing app data. The game does not upload movement traces or the full history of your runs. If you connect to Game Center, future run distances are also sent to Apple as described below. If you choose a rewarded ad, a limited run snapshot is sent for reward verification as described below.

Optional Game Center leaderboard

In the iPhone and iPad app, choosing Connect to Game Center lets the app send distances from future non-practice runs directly to Apple Game Center. Your Game Center nickname and best actual distance in whole metres are public on the leaderboard. Earlier saved records are not uploaded. The app stores your connection choice, Game Center player identifier, best pending distance and last submitted best on this device, separately for each Game Center account, so interrupted submissions can retry for their original account. Game Center leaderboard data is not sent to our wallet server.

Purchases and your wallet

The store uses a generated account identifier to verify purchases and maintain your purchased items. Our purchase service stores transaction identifiers, products, wallet changes, purchase status, and purchased gadget or upgrade usage needed to maintain the wallet. Native purchases are processed by Apple or Google and verified through RevenueCat. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history for validation, account functionality, and purchase analytics.

Recovery and support

Keep your store recovery code private: it can restore access to your purchased wallet. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to answer your request. Never send payment passwords or your recovery code by email.

Retention and your choices

If you have a store account, use Store → Store wallet & recovery → Delete account to delete the generated account and local progress on this device. This removes operational wallet data and recovery access, and requests deletion at the purchase provider. Provider deletion may finish later; it does not refund purchases. Limited transaction and deletion records are retained to prevent duplicate grants, handle purchase reversals and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact the publisher for access requests or deletion assistance. Hosting providers may process connection information to deliver and protect the service. Reward verification records are also used to prevent duplicate ad rewards. Deleting the generated account removes operational ad-reward records; limited opaque transaction markers remain to prevent replay. Clearing local progress does not itself delete records held by advertising or purchase providers. Deleting your store account or clearing local data does not erase records held by Apple Game Center. Manage Game Center records through Apple.

Clearing local progress

You do not need a store account to remove device-local progress. Export it from Settings first if you want a backup. Then clear this game's website data in your browser settings, or delete the native app and its local data using your device settings. Clearing local data does not delete a purchased wallet, request deletion at the purchase provider, or refund purchases.

Optional rewarded ads

Enabled native builds offer Google AdMob rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Before requesting an ad, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow where required. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices. The current integration requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. This does not mean that ads process no data.

Advertising data

Google's advertising SDK may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or app identifiers including advertising identifiers when available, network and device information, crash and performance diagnostics, and ad impressions and interactions. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and SDK operation. These practices are separate from the game's device-local progress storage.

Reward verification

When you request an ad reward, our service receives generated account and reward-request or run identifiers and the selected reward. Run rewards include distance traveled and Quanta collected; charge rewards include the chosen gadget. Google's signed completion callback includes transaction and verification data. We use these records to verify delivery and prevent duplicate rewards. Ad bonus Quanta and single-use charges are saved as earned progress on your device, separately from the purchased wallet. Reward verification does not upload the full gameplay simulation.

Device permissions

The game does not request GPS location, microphone, camera or contacts permissions. Network services and advertising providers can still process connection information, including IP-derived approximate location. Review available ad privacy choices and this notice before choosing an ad.
